# Trail Mapper

A phone web app for South Florida state and county park trails. It lists the trails near you, near any place you search, or across all of South Florida, and opens each trail on its own map with its details. Tap Start trail to see your live position, get warned when you drift off the path, and see the way back to parking along the trails. Tap End trail for a summary of your walk.

Trail lines come from the Florida Department of Environmental Protection, Office of Greenways & Trails "Existing Trails" inventory, loaded straight from the state's public map service and drawn exactly as published. Parking and restrooms come from OpenStreetMap when that service answers. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Open it in your phone's browser and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it. Trail lines and the map pieces you viewed are kept on the phone for use without signal.

Always follow park maps, signs and trail markers.
