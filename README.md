# Trail Mapper

A phone web app for South Florida park, preserve and nature trails. It lists the trails near you, near any place you search, or across all of South Florida, and opens each trail on its own map with its details. Tap Start trail to see your live position, get warned when you drift off the path, and see the way back to parking along the trails. Tap End trail for a summary of your walk.

Where the trail lines come from, each drawn exactly as published:

- Florida Department of Environmental Protection, Office of Greenways & Trails "Existing Trails" inventory: state, county, city, federal and nonprofit trails (paddling and motorized trails left out).
- Palm Beach County Environmental Resources Management, natural areas trail map, with the trail markers the county lists for each trail.
- OpenStreetMap, for nature areas that neither covers (wetlands and nature centers, for example). These are labeled OpenStreetMap in the app.

Parking and restrooms come from OpenStreetMap when that service answers. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Speed: the list loads a light copy of the trail data (names, lengths and rough positions) with all pages requested at once, shows a saved list right away, and loads a park's full trail lines only when you open it. Add `?bench` to the address to skip saved data and show load times; add `?bench&legacy` to time the previous way of loading for comparison.

Open it in your phone's browser and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it. Trail lines and the map pieces you viewed are kept on the phone for use without signal.

Always follow park maps, signs and trail markers.
